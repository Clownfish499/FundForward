About FundForward

FundForward is an academic software engineering project demonstrating how modern full-stack web platforms can integrate interpretable Machine Learning to strengthen trust in online crowdfunding.

Platform Mission

Empower individuals, educators, clinics, and community groups to raise support transparently while giving platform reviewers automated risk triage.

ML Transparency & Ethics

Our Random Forest model surfaces explainable risk factors (0–100 score) rather than black-box rejections. Human administrators always make the final decision.

Academic & Synthetic Data Disclosure: The fraud detection model is trained on a synthetic demonstration dataset (1,200 simulated campaign records). Risk scores (0–30 Low, 31–60 Medium, 61–100 High) are demonstration thresholds and probabilistic indicators—never legal proof of fraud. All donations on this platform are simulated and no real money is transferred.
