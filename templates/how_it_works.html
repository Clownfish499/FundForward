How FundForward Works

From campaign submission to ML risk scoring and simulated donor support.

For Creators

1. Create & Document

Register an account, define your fundraising goal, write a detailed narrative, and upload supporting verification documents.

ML Engine

2. Automated Fraud Screening

Our Scikit-learn Random Forest classifier extracts 9 features (target, description length, documents, account age, completeness, urgency keywords) and assigns a 0–100 risk score.

Admin Review

3. Verification & Approval

Administrators review the score and explainable reasons, then approve, reject, or request additional documentation.

For Donors

4. Simulated Support

Supporters browse verified campaigns, select preset or custom amounts, leave encouragement messages, and receive a simulated payment receipt.
